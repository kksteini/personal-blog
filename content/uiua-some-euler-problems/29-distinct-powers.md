## Problem

Consider all integer combinations of
$a^b$ for $2 \le a \le 5$ and $2 \le b \le 5$:

$$
\begin{array}{rl@{\qquad}rl@{\qquad}rl@{\qquad}rl}
2^2 &= 4    & 2^3 &= 8   & 2^4 &= 16  & 2^5 &= 32\\\\
3^2 &= 9    & 3^3 &= 27  & 3^4 &= 81  & 3^5 &= 243\\\\
4^2 &= 16   & 4^3 &= 64  & 4^4 &= 256 & 4^5 &= 1024\\\\
5^2 &= 25   & 5^3 &= 125 & 5^4 &= 625 & 5^5 &= 3125
\end{array}
$$

If they are then placed in numerical order, with any repeats removed, we get
the following sequence of $15$ distinct terms:
$$4, 8, 9, 16, 25, 27, 32, 64, 81, 125, 243, 256, 625, 1024, 3125.$$

How many distinct terms are in the sequence generated by $a^b$ for $2 \le a
\le 100$ and $2 \le b \le 100$?

## Easy for Uiua

Uiua is extremely handy for this problem.
For the example, notice that this is a table of powers defined by two ranges
from $2-5.$
We can visualize this with `bw join` on a `table`.

```uiua
    ⇡₂5 ⇡₂5
[2 3 4 5]
[2 3 4 5]

    ⊞˜⊂ ⇡₂5 ⇡₂5
╭─
╷ 2 2  2 3  2 4  2 5
╷ 3 2  3 3  3 4  3 5
  4 2  4 3  4 4  4 5
  5 2  5 3  5 4  5 5
                     ╯
```

> To get the same representation, we use `backward` on join.
> You can try skipping it to see what happens.

So, we see that the arguments at each table element are
the base and the power. Instead of `bw couple`, the table
function can simply be `pow`. Let's `trans` it to match
the problem example.

```uiua
    ⍉ ⊞ⁿ ⇡₂5 ⇡₂5
╭─
╷  4   8  16   32
   9  27  81  243
  16  64 256 1024
  25 125 625 3125
                  ╯
```

Yup. That matches perfectly.

At this point, we would simply `deshape` to flatten
the results into an array, `dedup` to remove duplicates
and then count them up with `len`.

```uiua
# Deshape
    ♭ ⍉ ⊞ⁿ ⇡₂5 ⇡₂5
[4 8 16 32 9 27 81 243 16 64 256 1024 25 125 625 3125]
# Dedup
    ◴ ♭ ⍉ ⊞ⁿ ⇡₂5 ⇡₂5
[4 8 16 32 9 27 81 243 64 256 1024 25 125 625 3125]
# Count
    ⧻ ◴ ♭ ⍉ ⊞ⁿ ⇡₂5 ⇡₂5
15
```

And that agrees with the example.

### Cleanup

We can modify `table` with `self` to skip
creating two ranges.

```uiua
    ⧻◴♭⍉ ˙⊞ⁿ ⇡₂5
15
```

And `trans` was just for show.

```uiua
    ⧻◴♭ ˙⊞ⁿ ⇡₂5
15
```

## Solution

We simply extend the range to $100$ from our example program.

```uiua
    ⧻◴♭ ˙⊞ⁿ ⇡₂100
9183
```
